# UI/UX Learning Overview

This track is designed for the scope of a **Lead UX Product Designer**: shaping enterprise experiences, setting UX direction, governing systems, validating decisions with research, and helping cross-functional teams deliver high-quality work.

UI is one part of UX. Learn visual and interaction craft, but prioritize the ability to turn complex user and business needs into simple, accessible, scalable experiences.

## Learning path

1. [UX Foundations](lesson/01-ux-foundations.md) — user-centered design, usability, mental models, cognitive load, and error recovery.
2. [User Research and Insight Integration](lesson/02-user-research.md) — research plans, usability testing, synthesis, hypotheses, and decision-making.
3. [Information Architecture and Systems Thinking](lesson/03-information-architecture.md) — navigation, taxonomy, task flows, journeys, and complex product structure.
4. [Interaction Design](lesson/04-interaction-design.md) — flows, control behavior, responsive patterns, and system states.
5. [Visual UI Design](lesson/05-visual-ui-design.md) — hierarchy, typography, spacing, color, grids, and data-heavy screens.
6. [Accessibility and Inclusive Design](lesson/06-accessibility.md) — WCAG, keyboard access, screen readers, focus, forms, contrast, and motion.
7. [Design Systems](lesson/07-design-systems.md) — tokens, components, patterns, documentation, governance, and design-to-code alignment.
8. [Enterprise UX](lesson/08-enterprise-ux.md) — roles, permissions, data density, approvals, auditability, and operational workflows.
9. [UX Product Strategy](lesson/09-product-strategy.md) — experience vision, outcomes, UX debt, roadmaps, and success measures.
10. [Cross-Functional Collaboration and UX Leadership](lesson/10-collaboration-and-leadership.md) — workshops, critiques, stakeholder alignment, mentoring, and team practices.
11. [Figma, Prototyping, and AI-Assisted Practice](lesson/11-figma-prototyping-and-ai.md) — scalable Figma work, prototypes, handoff, and responsible AI use.
12. [Portfolio Case Studies](lesson/12-portfolio-case-studies.md) — communicating strategic thinking, execution, collaboration, tradeoffs, and impact.

## Suggested capstone

Redesign a complex enterprise workflow, such as employee onboarding, appointment scheduling, claims processing, inventory management, or approvals. Work through personas, research plan, information architecture, task flows, wireframes, prototypes, accessible components, and an experience strategy. Turn the result into a portfolio case study that explains your decisions and measurable outcome.

## How to use each lesson

Read the concepts, answer the “Think through” prompts using one product problem, and produce the listed deliverable. Reusing the same capstone problem across the lessons will show how research, systems thinking, design craft, and leadership connect throughout a product lifecycle.
